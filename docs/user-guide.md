# Ikigai Compass — User Guide

## What is Ikigai?

Ikigai is a Japanese concept meaning "reason for being." It sits at the intersection of four elements:

- **What you love** — your passions and deep interests
- **What you are good at** — your skills and natural talents
- **What the world needs** — the problems and gaps you are positioned to address
- **What you can be paid for** — work that is sustainable and viable

Finding your Ikigai is about discovering where all four overlap.

## Getting Started

### 1. Create an account

Visit the landing page and click **Begin your journey** to sign up. You can also **Try without an account** to complete a short preview of the discovery flow using only your browser — your answers won't be saved until you register.

### 2. Complete the Discovery Flow

After signing in, go to **Discover** in the navigation. The discovery flow walks you through four themed prompts, one for each Ikigai dimension. Answer as freely and honestly as you can. Your responses can be edited at any time.

### 3. Generate Ikigai Hypotheses

Once you've saved your discovery session, go to **Hypotheses** and click **Generate hypotheses**. The AI will synthesize your answers into several candidate Ikigai directions, each with a title, explanation, and guiding question.

Review each hypothesis and mark the one that resonates most as your **primary direction**. You can regenerate hypotheses at any time.

### 4. Build a 90-Day Growth Plan

Navigate to **Plan** and create a new plan. Link it to your chosen hypothesis. Add:

- **Milestones** — 3–4 key outcomes you want to achieve over 90 days
- **Actions** — concrete steps that move you toward each milestone
- **Habits** — recurring practices that support your direction

### 5. Work with Your AI Coach

Go to **Coach** to start a coaching conversation. The AI coach knows your hypothesis and growth plan. Ask it anything — for accountability, reframing challenges, brainstorming next steps, or exploring fears.

### 6. Weekly Check-Ins

Every week, complete a **Check-In** to review what you planned, what you actually did, your energy level, and your satisfaction. The app calculates a **Momentum Score** (0–100) based on your responses. Progress snapshots are stored over time so you can track trends.

### 7. Track Obstacles

On the **Obstacles** page, log blockers you encounter — fears, resource gaps, skill gaps, or external factors. Mark them as resolved when you work through them.

### 8. Add Reflections

Use **Reflections** to capture journal-style insights at any point — things you learned, moments of clarity, questions that arose. Reflections can be linked to a specific hypothesis.

---

## Tips for Best Results

- Answer discovery prompts with specific examples, not abstractions.
- Re-run hypothesis generation after adding new reflections.
- Use the coach proactively — not just when stuck.
- Keep weekly check-ins short and honest. Precision beats polish.

---

## Privacy

Your discovery data, hypotheses, plans, and coaching conversations are private to your account. No data is shared with other users.
